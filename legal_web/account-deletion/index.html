Delete your account

Deleting your account is permanent. It removes your profile, all data you created in the app, files you uploaded, and your sign-in credentials. This page and the equivalent option inside the app perform the same deletion.

Guest accounts: if you never linked a Google or Apple identity, deleting from within the app (Settings) also deletes the anonymous account and its data.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record and sign-in credentials (including revoking linked providers).
  • Your profile and application data you created.
  • Files you uploaded.

Request deletion

Enter the email address associated with your account. We send a confirmation link; deletion runs after you confirm it.

This is a template page — the endpoint is not connected yet. Deletion becomes live with the backend deployment (see legal_web/README.md).
